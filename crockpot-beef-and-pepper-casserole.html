Crockpot Beef and Pepper Casserole

Source: Yummy Yield

Ingredients

  • 680 g lean ground beef
  • 15 ml olive oil
  • 1 medium yellow onion, finely diced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 large red bell pepper, diced
  • 1 large green bell pepper, diced
  • 1 large yellow bell pepper, diced
  • 1 can diced tomatoes, undrained (410 g)
  • 1 can tomato sauce (425 g)
  • 30 ml tomato paste
  • 240 ml low-sodium beef broth
  • 15 ml Worcestershire sauce
  • 5 g Italian seasoning
  • 5 g smoked paprika
  • 5 g garlic powder
  • 2.5 g onion powder
  • 5 g salt, or to taste
  • 2.5 g freshly ground black pepper
  • 1.25 g crushed red pepper flakes (optional)
  • 400 g cooked long-grain white rice
  • 200 g shredded sharp cheddar cheese, divided
  • 100 g shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 120 g sour cream (optional, for extra creaminess)
  • 30 g freshly chopped parsley
  • 30 g chopped green onions (optional)

Instructions

  1. Wash and dice the bell peppers into uniform, bite-sized pieces. Finely chop the onion and mince the garlic.
  2. Heat olive oil in a skillet over medium-high heat. Add ground beef and cook for 7–9 minutes until browned with no pink remaining.
  3. Stir in onion and garlic, cooking until the onion is translucent and garlic is fragrant, about 3–4 minutes.
  4. Grease the slow cooker and transfer the browned beef mixture into it.
  5. Scatter the diced peppers over the beef and stir to combine evenly.
  6. Pour in the diced tomatoes, tomato sauce, and beef broth, stirring until well combined.
  7. Mix in the Italian seasoning, smoked paprika, garlic powder, and other spices.
  8. Cover and set to cook on LOW for 4–5 hours or HIGH for 2–3 hours until tender.
  9. While the casserole cooks, prepare the rice according to the package instructions.
  10. Once the casserole is ready, gently stir in the cooked rice, ensuring it is evenly coated with the sauce.
  11. If using, add sour cream and mix thoroughly until creamy.
  12. Stir in half of the shredded cheese, allowing it to begin melting.
  13. Sprinkle the remaining cheese evenly over the top.
  14. Cover and cook for an additional 10–15 minutes on LOW until the cheese is thick and gooey.
  15. Turn off the crockpot and let it sit uncovered for 5–10 minutes so the sauce thickens slightly.
  16. Sprinkle with freshly chopped parsley and green onions, then serve.
