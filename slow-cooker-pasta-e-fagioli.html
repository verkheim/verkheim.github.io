Slow Cooker Pasta e Fagioli Soup

Source: dianaluis.com

Ingredients

  • 1 pound ground beef
  • 2 (15-ounce) cans kidney beans, drained and rinsed (or one can kidney beans and one can cannellini beans)
  • 1 (28-ounce) can diced tomatoes
  • 6 cups beef broth
  • 1 cup small pasta (ditalini, small shells, or elbow macaroni)

Instructions

  1. Brown the ground meat: Heat a large skillet over medium heat. Add the ground beef and break it up into small pieces with a wooden spoon. Cook until no pink shows and drain off the excess fat. Place the cooked beef in the slow cooker.
  2. Add tomatoes and beans: Drain and rinse the kidney beans and add them to the slow cooker. Add the diced tomatoes and their juice. Stir gently to evenly distribute the beans and tomatoes around the beef.
  3. Add the beef broth: Add the beef broth and stir once more. Cover and cook on LOW for 6 to 7 hours or HIGH for 3 to 4 hours.
  4. Add the pasta at the end: Add the uncooked pasta 20–30 minutes before serving. Cover and cook until al dente, checking after about 20 minutes. If the soup gets too thick after adding the pasta, add a splash of hot broth or water.
  5. Serve: Stir the soup one last time, taste, and adjust the seasoning if needed. Ladle into bowls and serve immediately, with grated Parmesan on top if you like.
