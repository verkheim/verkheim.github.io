Slow Cooker BBQ Pulled Chicken Sandwiches

Source: Food Fanatic

Prep Time: 5 min · Cook Time: 4 hrs on High or 8 hrs on Low (see Dave's notes — check much earlier)

Ingredients

  • 4–5 boneless, skinless chicken breasts (the five-pack bag)
  • 1 cup chicken stock (or broth)
  • 2 cups honey barbecue sauce, divided
  • Hamburger buns
  • Coleslaw (optional)

Instructions

  1. Rinse and pat dry the chicken breasts.
  2. Mix the chicken stock and 1/2 cup of the barbecue sauce together; pour into the slow cooker.
  3. Add the chicken, flipping a few times to coat.
  4. Cover and cook — 4 hours on High or 8 hours on Low (source times; see note below).
  5. Remove the chicken and pull apart with two forks.
  6. Stir the remaining 1-1/2 cups barbecue sauce into the pulled chicken and mix well.
  7. Toast the buns, pile on the chicken, top with coleslaw if using.
