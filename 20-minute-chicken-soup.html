20-Minute Chicken Soup

Source: EatingWell

Active Time: 20 mins · Total Time: 20 mins · Serves: 4

Ingredients

  • 1 tablespoon extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1 pound boneless, skinless chicken breasts, cut into 1/2-inch pieces
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 teaspoons salt-free garlic-and-herb seasoning blend
  • 1/2 teaspoon ground turmeric
  • 1/4 teaspoon crushed red pepper
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 8 cups unsalted chicken broth
  • 3 cups whole-wheat egg noodles
  • 1 cup frozen mixed vegetables
  • 2 cups chopped stemmed lacinato kale
  • 3 tablespoons lemon juice

Instructions

  1. Heat 1 tablespoon oil in a large Dutch oven over medium-high heat. Add chicken pieces; cook, stirring often, until no longer pink, about 5 minutes. Add minced garlic, 2 teaspoons seasoning blend, 1/2 teaspoon turmeric, 1/4 teaspoon crushed red pepper and 1/4 teaspoon salt; cook, stirring constantly, until fragrant, about 1 minute.
  2. Stir in 8 cups broth; cover and bring to a boil over high heat. Uncover and add 3 cups noodles and 1 cup frozen vegetables. Return to a simmer; reduce heat to maintain a gentle simmer. Cook, stirring occasionally, until the noodles are tender and the chicken is cooked through, about 7 minutes. Stir in 2 cups kale and 3 tablespoons lemon juice.
