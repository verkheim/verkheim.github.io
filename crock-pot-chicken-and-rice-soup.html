Crock-Pot Chicken and Rice Soup

Source: Lovely Food Blog

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 pounds boneless, skinless chicken breasts (about 2 to 3 breasts)
  • 3/4 cup long-grain white rice, uncooked
  • 2 cans (14.5 oz each) chicken broth
  • 1 can (10.5 oz) cream of chicken soup
  • 2 cups water
  • 3 medium carrots, peeled and sliced into coins
  • 3 stalks celery, sliced
  • 1 medium yellow onion, diced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 teaspoon dried thyme
  • 1 teaspoon dried parsley
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1 teaspoon salt, plus more to taste
  • 1 tablespoon butter

Instructions

  1. Layer the vegetables first: Add the sliced carrots, celery, and diced onion to the bottom of a 6-quart slow cooker. Scatter the minced garlic over the top.
  2. Add the chicken: Lay the chicken breasts on top of the vegetables in a single layer.
  3. Pour in the broth and soup: Pour both cans of chicken broth over the chicken. Spoon in the cream of chicken soup and add the 2 cups of water. Give everything a gentle stir around the edges to loosen up the cream of chicken soup and mix it into the liquid.
  4. Season the pot: Sprinkle in the dried thyme, dried parsley, black pepper, and salt. Drop the tablespoon of butter right on top of the chicken.
  5. Cook on LOW or HIGH: Cover and cook on LOW for 7 to 8 hours or on HIGH for 4 to 5 hours. The chicken is done when it reaches an internal temperature of 165°F and shreds easily with a fork. The vegetables should be completely tender.
  6. Shred the chicken: Use two forks to pull the chicken apart right in the pot. Stir the shredded chicken back through the soup.
  7. Add the rice: Stir in the uncooked long-grain white rice, making sure it is submerged in the liquid. Place the lid back on and cook on HIGH for an additional 30 to 45 minutes, until the rice is tender. Start checking at the 30-minute mark so the rice does not overcook.
  8. Taste and serve: Add more salt if needed. Ladle into bowls and serve right away with crackers or soft dinner rolls. The soup thickens as it sits; stir in a splash of warm water or broth to loosen it.
